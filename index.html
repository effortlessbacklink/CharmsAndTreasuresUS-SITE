About Us

CharmsAndTreasuresUS is a shop based in Palo Alto, California, offering a changing collection of vintage and antique pieces. From carved furniture and decorative art to glassware, tabletop items, and home accessories, we bring together special finds for different tastes and collections.

We believe that unique pieces can add character, charm, and a touch of history to any home. Whether you are decorating your space, searching for a thoughtful gift, or adding something special to your collection, our selection offers plenty of interesting items to explore.

Visit our official website to browse categories and discover available items. Each product links to its Etsy listing, where you can find photos, condition details, availability, and purchasing information. You can also find us on eBay, Facebook Marketplace, OfferUp, Mercari, and Depop.
